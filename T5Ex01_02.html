Hainanese Chicken Rice

This Singaporean staple is hands down one of my favorites, which says a lot given the multitude of over-the-top, tasty dishes that surround me.

Chicken rice is simple and soothing—exactly what you should order when you want to take a break from the usual fiery and heavy dishes.

>

Ingredients

For the Chicken

Ingredients Quantity
Whole Chicken 1.5 - 2kg
Bunch of Spring Onion 100g
Knob of Garlic 500g
Chicken Stock or Water 1 Cup

For the Rice

Ingredients Quantity
Thai Jasmine Rice 3 Cups
Chicken Fat 4 Tablespoon
Chicken Stock 800ml
Bundled Pandan Leaves 50g
  • 3 cups Thai jasmine rice
  • 4 tbsp chicken fat (fried with garlic and ginger)
  • 800 ml Chicken stock
  • A bundle of pandan leaves
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Clean the chicken and rub with salt to remove excess skin.
  2. Stuff the cavity with ginger and spring onions, then poach in stock.
  3. Prepare the rice by sautéing it with chicken fat before adding stock.
  4. Serve the chicken sliced at room temperature with the warm rice and chili sauce.
